# How to Pitch TruthLens (The 30-Second Elevator Pitch)
"TruthLens is a digital filter for student group chats. When a student receives a suspicious message (like 'Exams canceled tomorrow!'), they don't have to guess if it's real. They just forward it to our WhatsApp bot. Within seconds, the bot analyzes it using AI and automated rules, replying with a clear credibility score and warning signs. It keeps student groups safe, sane, and verified."

---

# Slide-by-Slide Presentation Guide (Simplified & Punchy)

## Slide 1: The Hook
* **Slide Title:** TruthLens: Sifting Truth from WhatsApp Rumors
* **What to Show:** A clean graphic of a WhatsApp message getting checked by a shield.
* **The Core Message:** A real-time, AI-backed helper that stops student group panic.
* **Speaker Script:** 
  > *"Every student has seen a message in their group chat claiming: 'Urgent! Exams are postponed!' or 'College is closed tomorrow!' Before anyone checks if it's true, it gets forwarded to ten other groups, causing massive panic. We built TruthLens to solve this instantly, right inside WhatsApp."*

---

## Slide 2: The Problem
* **Slide Title:** The "Forwarded as Received" Problem
* **What to Show:** A visual checklist of the issue:
  *  **Where students live:** WhatsApp/Telegram groups.
  *  **How fast rumors spread:** Instantly, without double-checking.
  *  **The hurdle:** Navigating complex college websites to verify info takes too long.
  *  **The result:** Missed exams, stress, and administrative chaos.
* **Speaker Script:** 
  > *"Students don't spread rumors maliciously; they do it because they are anxious and checking official portals is slow. We wanted to meet students where they already talk—on WhatsApp—and give them answers in 3 seconds."*

---

## Slide 3: How TruthLens Works (The "Three-Way" Check)
* **Slide Title:** Our Approach: The Triple Check
* **What to Show:** A simple diagram showing three puzzle pieces locking together:
  1.  **The Brain (AI):** Trained on thousands of real and fake student messages to recognize patterns.
  2.  **The Detective (Rules):** Looks for panic indicators like EXCESSIVE CAPS, "URGENT!!!" warnings, and missing official sign-offs.
  3.  **The Memory (Similarity):** Remembers previously flagged hoaxes so they can't be recycled.
* **Speaker Script:** 
  > *"Instead of relying on just one method, TruthLens runs a triple check. It uses Machine Learning to look at the context, Heuristic Rules to check the formatting, and database lookup to see if the rumor is a duplicate of a previously flagged hoax."*

---

## Slide 4: Simple Math Behind the Score
* **Slide Title:** How We Calculate Credibility
* **What to Show:** A simple progress bar mapping 0 to 100:
  *  **0 - 40:** Almost Certainly Fake / Rumor
  *  **41 - 60:** Suspicious / Verify First
  *  **61 - 100:** Likely Safe / Verified
* **The Formula Explained Simply:**
  * **70% of the score** comes from our AI model calculating how "real" the text sounds.
  * **30% of the score** is a penalty applied by our Heuristic scan if the message uses clickbait, panic words, or weird formatting.
* **Speaker Script:** 
  > *"We combine AI confidence and styling rules to give a clean score out of 100. If a message sounds official but is written in ALL CAPS with 10 emojis, our heuristics penalize the score, flagging it as suspicious."*
